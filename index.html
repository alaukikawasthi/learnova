About Learnova

Built for students and freshers, not just professionals.

Learnova started with a simple idea: most college students in India graduate strong on theory but short on the practical, job-ready skills recruiters actually look for. So we built short, live courses — taught by working professionals and supported by an AI learning assistant — that fit around your semester, internship or first job.

Every course ends with something real: a live project, a mock interview, a portfolio piece — not just a PDF certificate you never open again.

FAQ

Common questions from students.

Is Learnova beginner-friendly?

Yes. Most courses, including Web Development and Python Programming, assume no prior experience and build up from the fundamentals.

What courses are available?

AI & Machine Learning, Web Development, Python Programming, Data Science, Communication & Interview Skills, and Digital Marketing — with more added each term.

Can I learn at my own pace?

Live sessions follow a weekly schedule, but every class is recorded, so you can revisit lessons and catch up in your own time.

Are certificates available?

Yes, every course ends with a certificate of completion, along with a project or portfolio piece you can share with recruiters.

Is Learnova suitable for college students?

Yes. Batches run on evenings and weekends specifically so students can attend alongside their regular college schedule.

Do you offer placement or interview support?

Our Communication & Interview Skills course, and the career prep built into every course, cover resume reviews and mock interviews. We don't guarantee placements.
